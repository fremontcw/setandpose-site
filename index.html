Support

Import a gym video from Photos or Files. Review suggested posing photos or full-set training clips, choose what to keep, and save or share.

Choosing a video

Choose Posing or Training, then Choose from Photos or Choose from Files. You can record a video beforehand with your iPhone Camera. Once selected, tap Find my poses or Find my sets. Set & Pose keeps the original video unchanged.

Saving

Choose your photos or full-set clips, then tap Save to Photos. Allow Set & Pose to add photos and videos when prompted. Wait for the saved confirmation and check recently saved items in Photos. If permission was denied, allow adding in iPhone Settings or use Share.

Side poses

Tap a Side Chest or Side Triceps label to choose Left, Right or Side not specified. Left/right means your side toward the camera. Automatic pose suggestions may need correction and do not choose left/right for you.

Interrupted sessions

A finished posing review can be offered again when you reopen the app, including your labels and kept/saved choices. An unfinished processing run does not resume after the app is ended; choose the original video again to start a new review.
