Hit it. Rep it. Keep it.

Set & Pose finds your best poses and your full sets in the gym videos you already shoot. Real frames from your own video, picked on your iPhone. Never AI-enhanced.

Join the beta

Free during the beta. iPhone only, iOS 26 or later. Opens in Apple's TestFlight app.

Join on TestFlight

Posing

Import a posing video. Set & Pose pulls the clearest still of each mandatory pose, sorted into your division's slots, ready to save in 4:5 and 9:16. Correct any label with a long press.

Training

Import a training video. Each set becomes its own clip, first rep to last, with trim and brightness. Keep the ones you want and save them to Photos.

What stays private

Everything runs on your phone. Nothing you import or review leaves it unless you share it yourself. No account, no sign-in. Details in the privacy policy.

How to help

Use it before a show or a post day and tell us what it got wrong: the pose that landed in the wrong slot, the set that was really two, the moment you did not know what to tap. Send feedback from TestFlight or email setandpose.app@gmail.com.
