Privacy

Effective date: October 17, 2026.

Set & Pose analyzes gym videos on your iPhone to suggest posing photos and training clips. This beta requires no account or purchase. The app has no automatic media upload, advertising tracker or analytics SDK.

Your video and photos

Imports use Apple's Photos or Files picker. Saving requests permission to add selected outputs to Photos. Set & Pose does not request camera, microphone, full-library or location access and does not modify your original imported video.

Working files stay in protected local storage excluded from device backups. Body and face landmarks used during analysis are temporary and are not kept in reports. Set & Pose does not use face recognition to identify people.

A finished posing review may be retained locally for up to a week so you can continue it, including pose labels, kept photos and saved status. Done, Discard or starting a new session removes that review; expired reviews are removed when the app next performs cleanup. Other unfinished working sessions are cleaned up on relaunch. Settings remain on your device until changed or the app is deleted.

Copies you explicitly save to Photos or share are separate from temporary app files. Removing a review or deleting Set & Pose does not delete those copies. Manage them in Photos or the destination you chose. Those destinations may have their own backup, sync and privacy settings.

Optional evaluation exports

The optional Review Data feature creates a file on your device after you enter participant/session codes and confirm written consent. Posing exports contain photo picks, labels and evaluation details; selected posing photos are included only if you turn that option on. Training exports contain set timings and keep choices, without images or video. Set & Pose does not upload these files. You choose whether and where to share them in the iOS share sheet. Sending one is never required; some beta testers may be invited to share theirs to help check pose suggestions. If you choose to email one to support, the inbox and deletion practices under Feedback and TestFlight apply.

Feedback and TestFlight

When you contact support, your email and the details you choose to include are received in the Set & Pose Gmail inbox. The inbox is accessed only by the Set & Pose owner. Support messages are kept while an issue is unresolved and deleted within 12 months after it is resolved. Please do not attach photos or videos; describe the issue instead.

Apple separately processes TestFlight beta diagnostics and feedback under its TestFlight privacy information. This policy does not describe TestFlight as collecting no data.
